
# UK Food Hygiene Analysis

This is a deep analysis 


## Business problem.

Understanding food hygiene performance and patterns across
food establishments in the UK.


## Project objectives.

-  Clean and make the data ready for future assessments.
- Investigate food hygiene rating distributions, geographical variation, business categories and potential factors associated with lower ratings.


## Intended audience.

Public health organisations, local authorities,
researchers and consumers.


## Research questions.



## Scope and exclusions.

- Overall hygiene rating distribution
- Business category analysis
- Local authority comparisons
- Geographical patterns
- Data quality assessment


## Deliverables

- Reproducible Python analysis
- Data quality report
- Visualisations
- Key findings
- Final project documentation



## Known assumptions.

Scotland uses a different scheme (FHIS: Pass / Improvement Required), so ratings aren’t directly comparable with the 0-5 FHRS.
